# Empresa Saludable — Design Brainstorm

## Chosen Approach: "Botanical Sanctuary"

### Design Movement
Organic Minimalism meets Tropical Wellness — inspired by high-end spa branding, Japanese wabi-sabi philosophy, and Puerto Rico's lush natural landscape.

### Core Principles
1. **Breathing Space** — Generous whitespace, slow reveals, content that unfolds like nature
2. **Organic Geometry** — Rounded shapes, flowing curves, no harsh edges
3. **Sensory Warmth** — Colors, textures, and motion that feel tactile and calming
4. **Grounded Authenticity** — Real content, real pharmacies, real Puerto Rico

### Color Philosophy
A dual-palette blending soft calming tones with earthy grounding:
- **Primary Sage**: `#8B9E7C` — The core brand color, nature's calm
- **Warm Terracotta**: `#C4725F` — Earthy warmth, human touch
- **Cream Canvas**: `#FDF8F0` — Breathing room, linen-like warmth
- **Deep Forest**: `#2D3B2D` — Grounding, text, depth
- **Lavender Mist**: `#C4B5D4` — Gentle accent, wellness spirituality
- **Golden Hour**: `#D4A574` — Warmth, optimism, tropical light

### Layout Paradigm
Asymmetric flowing sections with organic dividers (wave SVGs, curved clip-paths). Content breathes with generous padding. Sections alternate between full-bleed imagery and contained text blocks.

### Signature Elements
1. **Floating Botanical Particles** — Subtle leaf/petal shapes drifting across sections
2. **Breathing Animations** — Elements that pulse gently like inhale/exhale (4s cycles)
3. **Organic Reveal Masks** — Content revealed through curved, flowing mask shapes on scroll

### Interaction Philosophy
Every interaction should feel like a gentle exhale — smooth, unhurried, calming. Hover states bloom softly. Scroll reveals are gradual. Nothing jolts or startles.

### Animation
- GSAP ScrollTrigger for section reveals with organic easing
- Lenis smooth scroll for buttery navigation
- Breathing-pace keyframe animations on accent elements
- Staggered text reveals with 50ms delays
- Parallax on botanical imagery at 0.3x speed

### Typography System
- **Display**: Playfair Display (serif) — elegant, editorial wellness feel
- **Body**: DM Sans — clean, modern, highly readable
- **Accent**: Space Grotesk — for labels, tags, small caps

### Brand Essence
"Empresa Saludable is Puerto Rico's integrated wellness network connecting communities to pharmacies, health services, and holistic wellbeing — for health-conscious Puerto Ricans who deserve beautiful, accessible healthcare information."
Personality: Nurturing, Sophisticated, Grounded.

### Brand Voice
Headlines sound like a gentle invitation, not a command. CTAs feel like a warm suggestion.
- "Tu bienestar comienza aquí" (not "Sign up now!")
- "Encuentra tu farmacia" (not "Search pharmacies")

### Wordmark & Logo
An abstract leaf-human figure merging into a circular organic shape. Sage green and terracotta. Represents the union of nature and human wellness.

### Signature Brand Color
Sage Green `#8B9E7C` — unmistakably this brand's color. Calming, natural, Puerto Rico's lush vegetation.

### Music
Bossa Nova background — soft, calming Brazilian jazz that complements the tropical wellness atmosphere. Plays on user opt-in with a floating music toggle.
