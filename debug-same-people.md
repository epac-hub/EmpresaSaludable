# Problem Analysis: "Same People"

The user says the people in the section background images look the same. Looking at all 4:

1. **Pilares** - Group meditating: all have similar olive/tan skin, dark hair, 30s-40s, similar body types
2. **Cumplimiento** - Boardroom: same "type" of people - similar skin tone, dark hair, 40s-50s
3. **Planes** - Rooftop cocktail: same "type" again - all look like the same AI face template
4. **Farmacias** - Pharmacy: woman with dark hair + older man with gray hair

The AI keeps generating the same "generic Latin person" face. The user wants VISIBLY DIFFERENT people across sections.

## Solution: 
Instead of trying to get "different people" from the same AI (which keeps producing similar faces), I should:
1. Use completely different SCENES that don't focus on faces/people as much
2. Or use abstract/architectural luxury images without people
3. Or specify VERY different physical characteristics (different races, ages, body types, hair colors)

## New approach: 
Generate images that are more about the ENVIRONMENT/CONCEPT and less about faces:
- Pilares: Abstract luxury wellness architecture (no people, just beautiful spa/nature)
- Cumplimiento: Modern office interior with documents/screens (minimal people)
- Planes: Luxury event setup/venue (empty or distant people)
- Farmacias: Beautiful pharmacy interior (architectural focus)
